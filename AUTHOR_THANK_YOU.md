# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Yang Zhao, Jungeun Lee, Jeong hwan Jeon, and Sze Zheng Yong for
the clear formulation of hard constraint-affine neural networks and for
making the theorem statements and architectural mechanisms precise enough for

authors' contribution while keeping proof evidence, finite corroboration,
